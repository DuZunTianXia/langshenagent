The phone app catches up with the desktop: it always says whether the computer is connected, editing a sent message is the same full sheet as on the desktop, and a subagent's card opens to its live conversation.

New
- Connection status everywhere on the phone: a banner while it connects or the computer is unreachable, the home title's dot green, grey or red, and sessions open again on their own once the link is back.
- Editing a sent message on the phone is the desktop's sheet: the text, the file changes to roll back picked from the revert plan, and the model, mode and permission chips.
- A subagent's card opens to its conversation live, as on the desktop: it updates as it works and can be stopped from the card while it runs.

Fixed
- The phone no longer spins forever on a session list or a conversation when the computer is offline: it says so and offers to reconnect.
